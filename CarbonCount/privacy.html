Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 26, 2024

1. Information We Collect

Account Information:

  • Email address (verified, non-disposable)

Usage Data:

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide and improve our services

3. Data Storage & Security

Storage: Data is stored on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) with servers in asia-south1 (Mumbai).

Security Measures:

  • End-to-end encryption in transit (TLS/SSL)
  • Encrypted storage at rest
  • Role-based access control
  • Regular security audits
  • Firewall and DDoS protection

4. Data Sharing

We DO NOT sell your data.

We may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Firebase (Google), payment processors
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger/acquisition

5. Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Rectify: Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete: Request account and data deletion
  • Port: Export your data in JSON format
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails
  • Restrict: Limit processing of your data

To exercise rights: Email privacy@carboncount.com

6. Cookies & Tracking

Essential Cookies: Session management, authentication

Analytics: Google Analytics (anonymized IP)

Control: You can disable cookies in browser settings, but this may affect functionality.

7. Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: Data retained while account is active
  • Deleted Accounts: Data deleted within 30 days
  • Legal Holds: Data retained if required by law
  • Backups: Backup data purged within 90 days

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover such data, it will be deleted immediately.

9. International Transfers

Data may be transferred to countries outside your jurisdiction. We ensure adequate safeguards through:
